Samguk Shorts Uploader

Samguk Shorts Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the creator of the history channels 진짜 삼국지 (@realsamguk) and 本当の三国志 (@realsangokushi). The channels publish one short video a day comparing famous scenes of the novel Romance of the Three Kingdoms with the historical records.

What the tool does

  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes their own accounts.
  • The tool uploads the creator's own finished videos with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings the creator chooses (Content Posting API).
  • The tool reads the creator's own profile and public video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) to help plan future videos (Display API).

The tool is used only by the channel owner for the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public.
