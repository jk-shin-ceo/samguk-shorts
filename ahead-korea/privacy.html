Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what data Ahead Korea Uploader (Google OAuth app "PoliticsNews1", "the App") handles. The App is used only by the channel owner for the owner's own YouTube channel "Ahead Korea".

Data we access

  • YouTube channel information (channel ID, title) to confirm which channel is connected.
  • The channel's own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments) via the YouTube Data API.
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained, traffic sources).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by Google, needed to upload videos and read statistics.

How we use it

  • To upload the owner's own videos to the owner's channel.
  • To analyze the performance of the owner's own videos and improve future videos.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private computer and are never sold or shared with third parties. Data is not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channel is active. Revoking the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops all access; tokens are then deleted. To request deletion of any stored data, email repplai2@gmail.com.
