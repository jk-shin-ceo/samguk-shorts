Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what data Samguk Shorts Uploader ("the App") handles. The App is used only by the channel owner for the owner's own TikTok accounts.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Profile statistics (follower count, like count, video count) and the list of the account's own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, needed to upload videos and read statistics.

How we use it

  • To upload the owner's own videos with the settings the owner chooses.
  • To analyze the performance of the owner's own videos and improve future videos.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored in the owner's private storage and are never sold or shared with third parties. Data is not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channels are active. Revoking the App's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) stops all access; tokens are then deleted. To request deletion of any stored data, email pooq99@gmail.com.
