Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what data Samguk Shorts Uploader ("the App") handles. The App is used only by the channel owner for the owner's own TikTok accounts and the owner's own five YouTube channels (진짜 삼국지, Real Three Kingdoms, 本当の三国志, 三國真相, Tam Quốc Sự Thật).

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API the App accesses, only for the owner's own channels: channel information (channel ID, title, subscriber count), the channel's own videos, playlists and their metadata and public statistics, comments on the channel's own videos (to post the channel's first comment and replies), and channel analytics reports (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained, audience retention). OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the owner's private storage.

The App does not collect data from other YouTube users, except the public text and display name of comments left on the owner's own videos, which are read only to reply to them and are kept in a private log. No YouTube data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The App does not place cookies or similar technology on any user's device.

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, and all tokens and stored YouTube data are deleted within 7 days after access is revoked or on request to the contact address below.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Profile statistics (follower count, like count, video count) and the list of the account's own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, needed to upload videos and read statistics.

How we use it

  • To upload the owner's own videos with the settings the owner chooses.
  • To analyze the performance of the owner's own videos and improve future videos.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored in the owner's private storage and are never sold or shared with third parties. Data is not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channels are active. Revoking the App's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) stops all access; tokens are then deleted. To request deletion of any stored data, email pooq99@gmail.com.
